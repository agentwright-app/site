This policy explains what personal data agentwright.app (the "site") and purchases of Agentwright Starter involve, and how we handle it. "We" and "us" means Agentwright. Questions or requests: [hello@agentwright.app](mailto:hello@agentwright.app).

## What we collect

**Visits.** We count visits without cookies and without ad trackers. Each page sends a small anonymous ping with the page path, the site you came from and the `utm_source` label of the link you followed, if it had one. We also count clicks on the checkout link and on links to the code preview, as daily totals per page that are not tied to you. To count unique visitors per day we keep a one-way hash of your IP address and browser name for at most two days, then it is deleted. To tell automated traffic from people we also keep a daily total of new visitors per network operator (for example an internet provider or a cloud host), which is not tied to you. We never store your raw IP address.

**Email list.** If you leave your email address on the site, we store it with the page you signed up from, the referring page and your country (from your connection). We use it only to send the emails you signed up for: Agentwright updates such as new releases, or Claude API price change alerts. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email or writing to us.

**Blog comments.** If you comment, we store the name you enter, your comment and your country, and publish the name and comment. To limit spam we keep a one-way hash of your IP address for 10 minutes.

**Purchases.** Payment is handled by our merchant of record, Polar, which processes your payment details under its own [privacy policy](https://polar.sh/legal/privacy-policy). We never see your card details. Polar shares your order number, email address and name with us so that we can issue your license key, email you the download link, and answer support requests.

**Emails you send us.** If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you and to help with later questions.

## How we use it

Only to run the site, deliver and support the Starter, prevent abuse, and send you the emails you asked for. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who processes it for us

- **Cloudflare** hosts the site and stores visit counts, the email list, comments and license records.
- **Polar** processes payments and taxes as merchant of record.
- **Resend** sends our transactional emails, such as your license key.
- **Google (Gmail)** hosts our support inbox.

These providers may process data outside your country. They act under their own security and privacy commitments.

## How long we keep it

Visit hashes: up to two days. Comment rate-limit hashes: 10 minutes. Email list entries: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. License and order records: as long as your license is active and as long as tax and accounting rules require. Support emails: as long as needed to help you.

## Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at [hello@agentwright.app](mailto:hello@agentwright.app). We answer within 30 days. Deleting a license record ends access to updates for that license. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
